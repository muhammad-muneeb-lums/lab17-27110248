How Chai Khana started

One late night on MM Alam Road in 2019, our founder was looking for a proper cup of chai after a long evening. The tea was there, but a warm table and fresh paratha were harder to find.

So Chai Khana began with a simple idea: keep the kettle on, cook with care, and make room for anyone whose day runs past midnight.

What we make fresh every night

We knead our dough fresh for the night’s parathas. Our chai masala is blended in-house, then added to each pot as it brews.

And the nihari stock gets time on a slow simmer, so it’s ready when the late-night hunger arrives. Fresh, steady, and made here.

The night crew

Four of us keep Chai Khana going from 6pm to 6am: one on chai, one on the paratha tawa, one tending the nihari, and one looking after aap.

We work as a team through the quiet hours and the busy ones. There’s always someone ready to pour another cup.

Come hungry, stay for chai. We’ll save you a seat.
